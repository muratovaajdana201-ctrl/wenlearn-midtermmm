FAQ

Frequently asked questions about WebLearn. Click a question to see the answer.

WebLearn is a learning website for the Web Technologies-1 course at Astana IT University. It has ten lectures, a quiz after every lecture, live coding tasks and a Final Test.

Open the quiz of a lecture, choose your answers and press Check Answers. The correct answers turn green, wrong choices turn red and every question has a short explanation. Press Try again to start over.

Every lecture has three small coding tasks. Each task has requirements and an expected result. You write the code yourself and can open the Show Solution button to compare.

The Final Test has 27 questions, three for every lecture. The pass mark is 70%. After you press Check Answers, you see your score in percent and an explanation for every question.
